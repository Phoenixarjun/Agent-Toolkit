# Opportunity and hiring evidence

Use current external sources only for live market claims. Prefer official company careers and public ATS pages; job boards are discovery leads. Access only permitted sources or authorized connectors; no CAPTCHA bypass, account scraping, bulk unsolicited contact, credential use without consent. Check actual job page, employer identity, requisition, location, status, checked time and any stated publication/closing date. A live HTTP response alone does not prove an opening; inspect closure markers, redirects, current listing and page content. If inaccessible, report unknown; no guessed URL, missing salary or date. Old/reposted listings warrant caution, not accusations of ghost hiring. A live listing does not prove active headcount.

Listing labels: EMPLOYER_LISTED (direct current listing observed), AGGREGATOR_ONLY, INACCESSIBLE, CLOSED, UNCLEAR. Record posting age separately (not stated if absent). Recheck stale evidence before candidate applies. For job matching extract required vs preferred vs ambiguous qualifications; map evidence as SUPPORTED, PARTIAL, NOT_SHOWN, UNKNOWN. Hard constraints MEETS, CONFLICTS, UNCLEAR; confirmed nonnegotiable conflict overrides fit. Verdict PURSUE/HOLD/SKIP with reason and next action; uncertainty can produce HOLD. Numeric ranking optional only with transparent candidate-side dimensions, never hiring probability or universal ATS score.

Company research triangulates official product and business information, filings when available, dated announcements, engineering blogs/repos, relevant public team signals and multiple independent sources. Employee reviews, anonymous accounts and candidate reports are directional, biased and not proof of culture. Avoid invasive research into individuals. Separate team/role-specific evidence from company-wide assumptions. Report compensation by location, role and date, with distinction between disclosed, cited market range, candidate target and unknown. Don't invent salary or funding estimates. For scams flag observable signals (payment demand, inconsistent domain, suspicious identity, unusual credential requests) and verify through official channels; avoid unsupported accusations.

Application: maintain real dates, titles and evidence, tailor terminology where genuinely equivalent; never insert missing keywords as experience. Prefer simple, readable, selectable text unless user requests another format. No claim of ATS pass without specific parse evidence; test extraction/order after actual document export if tools exist. Compare submitted version to canonical evidence and public profile only within authorized access. Candidate submits/sends; tracker update needs actual confirmation. Optional recruiter outreach is genuine draft with truthful relationship context, never mass messaging.

Interview: separate preparation from mock assessment. Use JD, stage, submitted resume, target role and confirmed company evidence. Distinguish likely topics from known process. Ask questions appropriate to role, let user answer before revealing solutions in mock mode, and avoid pretending candidate supplied an answer. Use panel-specific independent notes, exact observed evidence and prioritized next drills. Offer support uses actual terms, explicit assumptions, equivalent periods, base vs guaranteed vs target bonus vs equity; disclose liquidity/vesting and uncertainty, avoid legal/tax certainty without appropriate verification. Candidate chooses and acts.

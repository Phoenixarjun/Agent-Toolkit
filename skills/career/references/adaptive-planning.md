# Adaptive planning contract

The plan is a blueprint toward a target, not a fixed course list. Separate stable pillars, role-specific capabilities and adaptive execution. Foundations vary by profession and current competence. Derive them from target role and demonstrated gaps, not generic technology shopping. Example for a long-horizon engineering transition: scalable backend, distributed systems, data/storage, networking and OS where relevant; specialize later in AI systems, retrieval, evaluation, inference or role-specific work. A designer or salesperson needs different durable pillars. Near-term candidates exploit existing foundations instead of restarting a curriculum.

## Intake and readiness

Recover known facts; ask grouped questions in at most two initial rounds (up to 5 material questions per round), follow-up only for a blocker or by request. Ask about current role/status, target, timeline/deadline, available hours, hard constraints, demonstrable accomplishments, readiness gaps and platform preferences. Current compensation optional. Assess multiple dimensions separately: foundations, role skills, evidence/portfolio, profile/materials, market alignment, interview readiness and practical constraints. Record basis, unknowns and priority; never equate public visibility or a percentage with hireability. Public footprint audit is optional and identity-confirmed; record access failures separately from absent evidence.

## Horizon and capacity

Start from deadline and actual weekly capacity, account for existing commitments, availability and contingency. For multi-month plans reserve roughly 10–20% of future time unless user constraints justify another buffer. Use near-term actionable milestones, later outcome-level targets; avoid fixed technology choices 12 months ahead. Prioritize durable foundations for long horizons when genuinely missing. For 6–12 months combine selective foundations, specialization, evidence and professional presence; for 1–3 months emphasize readiness, targeted applications and existing proof; for days/weeks prioritize current interviews or urgent opportunities. Active search timing follows notice period, hiring lead time and urgency, not a universal three-month rule. Early market sampling can guide priorities without starting applications. Use learning plans only where needed, not as the default career product. Respect existing project choices; do not mandate new projects or public content. Select a small portfolio of strongest existing evidence (often 2–3, at most 4 recommended flagship projects unless requested); if absent, propose targeted options and scope to capacity. Never promise completed future work.

## Plan record

Use JSON schema version 1 (see optional `scripts/career/plan_store.py` for structural checks). Each immutable plan includes `plan_id`, `parent_plan_id` (null first version), `created_at`, `target`, `start_date`, `deadline`, `weekly_hours`, `buffer_fraction`, `review_interval_days`, `pillars` (id, outcome, rationale), `capabilities` (id, outcome, pillar_ids), `milestones` (id, title, capability_ids, start_date, due_date, estimated_hours, depends_on, acceptance, workflow_id (registered workflow ID or null for candidate-only work), activation ACTIVE | SCHEDULED | CONDITIONAL, trigger), `assumptions`, `evidence_refs`, `review_rationale`. A revised plan schedules remaining work from its new start date; completed prior milestones stay in history and progress rather than being rescheduled. Keep progress separate keyed by stable milestone ID, with completed evidence and dates. Do not move a completed milestone backward without explicit correction; preserve history if a milestone is retired. Plan approval record identifies exact proposed version, candidate decision and date. Active pointer changes only after approval. Text mode uses the same logical fields.

Show a realistic capacity check: sum estimated hours against available time minus reserve; check dependency order, overlapping workload by planning period, earliest realistic milestones, deadlines, and unavailable prerequisites. Unknown capacity is provisional; ask before treating a schedule as committed. Identify risk, fallback and clear evidence of completion per milestone. Do not assert a date is feasible solely because it fits a calendar.

## Reality review and versioning

At each full invocation compare actual date, due review, progress, target and new triggers. Suggested intervals: 12–18 months 60–90 days; 6–12 months 30–60 days; 3–6 months ~30 days; 1–3 months 14–28 days if useful; immediate interview on material event. Choose and persist interval; manual earlier review on requested material change, deadline risk, new target, unexpected offer, change of employment or credible external signal. No autonomous wakeup. Optional scheduling is a separate supported and authorized action.

Market analyst gathers dated, role/location/seniority-relevant sources: official job descriptions and hiring documentation, independent employer sample, production adoption and original research. Compare to prior baseline and record coverage and selection bias. Distinguish observation from inference, cross-source corroboration, weak trend and isolated opinion. A popular claim is not proof. An inaccessible source is unknown, not evidence of no change. Verify external evidence through existing research or fresh independent review. Do not demand impossible 100% certainty; demand proportionate evidence for cost and reversibility.

Plan critic evaluates actual materiality and timing: target relevance, strength of evidence, cost, remaining time, dependency impact, completed work reuse, opportunity cost, changed milestones, risk of doing nothing, reversibility and fallback. Preserve durable pillars by default, but revise them if actual target or evidence warrants. Produce `KEEP` or `PROPOSE` or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. For PROPOSE, include plan A (retain) and plan B (candidate revision), exact diff, affected downstream records, capacity comparison, tradeoffs and recommendation. Never automatically replace active plan. Candidate approval of exact version required; if stale parent changed, rebase and ask again. Record review even when unchanged; next due date derives from last completed review (or initial approval date if no review yet), not failed research attempt. Plan revision may be justified by changed candidate constraints without market research. Reassess actual work on every resume; retain earlier versions and decisions.

## Recurring and due work

A scheduled milestone becomes ready when its start date has arrived, dependencies are evidenced complete and no blocker exists. Its immutable activation label is not rewritten. Conditional work additionally requires an observed trigger; record `activation_confirmed_at` and `activation_evidence_refs` in progress after candidate confirmation or authorized evidence. Neither elapsed time nor a draft counts as a trigger. Overdue unfinished work stays visible; flag deadline risk rather than silently reschedule.

For candidate-approved ongoing activity (e.g. weekly LinkedIn research/drafting, periodic GitHub documentation, blog cadence), record a routine separately from immutable milestones under `strategy/routines.json`. Link each routine to the active approved plan and its allocated milestone capacity; account for expected repetitions in the original capacity budget, not as extra free hours. Record stable ID, plan ID, workflow ID, title, cadence in days, next due date, status ACTIVE/PAUSED/ENDED/BLOCKED and prior occurrence references. The candidate chooses cadence and channel; no universal weekly posting requirement. Candidate-only routines may use null workflow ID. At invocation, pick at most one overdue occurrence per routine unless the candidate wants backlog catch-up; skip obsolete missed occurrences with an explicit record. Do not create duplicate drafts for the same occurrence; resume an unfinished occurrence first. Advance next due only when the defined occurrence acceptance is met or the candidate explicitly skips it. A completed draft is distinct from confirmed publication. Reuse existing topic dossiers and check freshness; each completed occurrence gets its own dated run ID. Keep routine progress distinct from milestone completion. On plan change, reconcile carried routines, capacity and next due with user approval; do not silently migrate them. A review reminder or routine does not schedule autonomous execution.

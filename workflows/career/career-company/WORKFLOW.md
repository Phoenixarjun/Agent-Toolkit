# Company Reality Check

Load opportunity-and-hiring, workspace, delegation and rubric. Confirm exact employer, geography/team/role and candidate decision context. For material multi-company comparison assign separate `career-company-investigator` contexts, reuse existing research workflow for deep external verification. Inspect original dated sources on business/product, actual hiring, engineering, funding/financial health when public, company changes, role/team fit, compensation signals and relevant work conditions. Verify claims and distinguish anecdotes/inference; report unavailable sources, contradicting evidence and selection bias. No private data investigation or unsupported scam/culture judgments. Critic evaluates evidence coverage and decision usefulness; no forced negative findings. Return company report: executive decision context, evidence table with URLs/dates, business and technical reality, role/team unknowns, positive/risk signals, compensation source types, unresolved questions for recruiter, recommendation (continue diligence, pursue, hold or avoid for stated confirmed issue). No employer contact or automatic application.

# Resume & Application Materials

Read workspace, opportunity-and-hiring, delegation and rubric. Establish whether master, role baseline, audit, JD-specific tailor, cover/outreach draft or tracker update. Recover exact candidate evidence and existing materials; verify JD status if supplied/available; do not require job discovery or full plan for direct resume task. Clarify missing material claims; no unsupported keyword stuffing. Reuse role match and company report if available.

Assign `career-resume-strategist` with exact scope and requirement matrix. For master baseline use actual career evidence; for tailored select strongest role-specific bullets and explicit gaps. `career-content-editor` may improve voice/structure without adding claims. Independent `career-evidence-verifier` checks final material statements, arithmetic, provenance and confidentiality; `career-recruiter` or `career-critic` assesses readability, fit and interview defense, not hypothetical ATS rank. Keep exact official titles/dates and candidate facts. Reverify edits; at most two rounds. For export, use authorized available document renderer; verify text extraction, reading order and layout when actually possible, otherwise deliver Markdown/plain draft and say export untested. No invented ATS certification.

Output exact draft(s), before/after if useful, requirement-to-evidence map, claim disposition, missing evidence/keywords genuinely absent, review results and manual submission checklist. Track prepared version with JD snapshot, evidence revision and status only if authorized. APPLIED requires candidate confirmation of actual submission. Draft outreach only if requested and truthful. Do not send, fill live forms or submit. No silent profile/ledger mutation.

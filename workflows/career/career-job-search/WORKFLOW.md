# Job Discovery & Matching

Read opportunity-and-hiring, workspace, delegation and rubric. Recover target roles, geography/remote, seniority, hard constraints and time. For a supplied JD, skip broad discovery and verify source if possible. For live search, split distinct source/query assignments among `career-job-scout` instances if helpful; use authorized public pages/ATS tools, not fabricated URL or hidden scraping. Record checked times, posting evidence, status, unknowns and coverage. Deduplicate by employer requisition. Direct employer listing observed does not prove headcount confirmation. Keep aggregator-only or inaccessible leads separate from verified live openings; use HOLD pending confirmation when liveness is material. Exclude closed or confirmed nonnegotiable conflict from recommended pursuit; keep provenance.

Assign `career-opportunity-analyst` per candidate role snapshot (batch by runtime, prevent cross-JD contamination). Map requirements and hard constraints; verdict PURSUE/HOLD/SKIP with rationale and uncertainty. Prioritize by user goals and effort, not pretend employer score. Candidate can request `career-company` for deeper investigation before tailoring; do not automatically research every company or submit. Recheck freshness when shortlisted for application. Propose candidate-confirmed tracker events, no silent changes from scraped content. Output sorted actionable shortlist with official URLs where verified, dates/status, requirement match, blockers, missing info, next action, search limitations, and no-results honest result. No invented openings or ongoing monitoring.

# LinkedIn Profile Optimization

Read workspace, delegation, content-and-platforms and rubric. Recover user-provided or confirmed authorized profile, scope, target audience, evidence and voice. Inspect existing headline, About, experience, projects, Featured, skills, links and activity only where relevant/accessible. If direct section request, inspect only requested section and minimal consistency context. Reuse current audit or dispatch `career-presence-auditor`; record missing visibility. No account edits.

1. Freeze common positioning brief and evidence snapshot. Identify material sections needing improvement. Spawn isolated `career-presence-optimizer` instances per distinct selected section, each with section-specific rubric and shared fact slice. Avoid rewriting effective sections; parallel where supported.
2. Assemble proposed replacements, verify material claims via fresh `career-evidence-verifier` (batch per section with exact IDs, isolate from authors). Check metric basis, ownership, titles, wording and confidentiality. Remove or qualify unsupported claims. Do not penalize reasonable supported user-reported facts.
3. Send assembled profile or requested section(s) to `career-content-editor` for voice and cross-section coherence; editor cannot increase claim strength. Critique independently with 2–3 role-relevant lenses when full audit warrants (`career-recruiter`, `career-critic` technical/hiring audience; use one for narrow task). Freeze rubric and ask defensibility questions; PASS allowed. Reverify final changes and ensure consistency with untouched sections where visible; limit corrections to two.
4. Provide ready-to-paste text in section order, before/after, reasoning, claim ledger, links/assets and questions requiring evidence. State preserved sections, visibility limits, platform constraints actually verified, and exact update instructions. Candidate edits manually; no posting frequency, discoverability or hiring guarantees.
